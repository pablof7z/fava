### D2 — Where does the admission window live? ⏸ ANSWER INBOUND

and freezes the cohort at admission, in its router admit path. Our ledger gives
`fava-observe` logical demand and the desired plan, makes the planner pure, and
makes transport the executor — which puts the window in `fava-observe`.

I have asked both the subscriptions and observe agents to argue this
independently and will reconcile. Escalates to you only if they disagree.

### D3 — `impl QuerySource for Fava`: router acquisition

Routers currently re-enter the whole engine to answer a routing question, and
start from a **fabricated empty snapshot**, so route revision 1 is empty even
with a warm cache. Three audit findings land here. It also cannot label itself
honestly: a nested Fava composes all three source roles but claims `EventCache`
while emitting write-store events.

- **(a) Recommended.** Delete it; give routers a narrow, non-recursive local-query
  service. Matches ARCHITECTURE.md's Router direction rule; kills recursion,
  fabricated initial, and the composition problem together.
- (b) Add `SourceKind::Composed`. Honest evidence, recursion stays.
- (c) Explicit-only router acquisition. No recursion by construction, but the
  outbox loses locally cached relay lists.

### D4 — WRITE-027: is a total router refusal terminal?

After the routing fix, a write whose routers all refuse settles terminal
`NoDestination`. The publication owner reads that as contradicting WRITE-027.
Two owners, one disputed transition; neither agent would overrule the other.

- (c) Distinguish a healthy chain that reports no destination (terminal) from one

# 3. THE nmp GROUPING MODEL (authoritative for subscription work)

Full report: `.planning/audit/2026-08-23/nmp-filter-merge-comparison.md`.

nmp **never rewrites a sent subscription.** Demand batches in a fixed 10 ms,
first-arrival-anchored, **non-sliding** window (sliding starves under steady
arrival). Freeze happens at admission, before the socket write; the merge compiles
the cohort against an **empty** incumbent namespace, so it is structurally
incapable of widening a live REQ.

Post-freeze demand takes one of three paths: exact attach (refcount bump only);
attach to a physically-covering incumbent via a real containment test, with
limited requests exact-only; or a new REQ carrying its **full** filter — nmp
deliberately does not subtract incumbent coverage, because subtraction is unproven
and causes under-fetch. Withdrawal decrements and closes at zero; the survivor
keeps its over-broad filter and local re-filtering discards the surplus. A
post-EOSE joiner is seeded from the local store and retro-credited.

Divergences: **1 critical, 5 high, 4 medium, 5 low.** The critical one cuts both
ways — **withdrawal has the same close-and-reopen bug as join.**

We are **already sound** on cross-product refusal, the `limit` rule including the
equal-limits subtlety, `since`/`until` equality, and `search` equality. We are
**ahead of nmp** on three points: the `default_filter_limit` guard (nmp lacks it
and has the under-fetch bug it guards elsewhere), the five-declared-limit model
with typed shortfall, and the differential testkit. **Two items to send back to
nmp** are in §C.17 of the report.
