## Required RED and Deliberate Breaks

1. Remove one selected host during lowering: multi-host acquisition/provenance evidence fails.
2. Change content queries to `only_from_relays`: accepted-local visibility fails.
3. Change record queries to `from_relays`: local-only false authority enters.
4. Collapse projection by group id or field-merge metadata: fork disagreement evidence fails.
5. Add canonical-host selection: public API/prohibition test fails.
6. Remove one publication destination: one wire witness lacks its exact EVENT handoff.
7. Rebuild a signed event or accept missing/duplicate/contradictory `h`: identity or zero-custody/zero-wire evidence fails.
8. Drop malformed/oversized rows or truncate a projection: exact accounting/bounds evidence fails.
9. Add a forbidden lifecycle dependency or universal `39000`/`h` switch: architecture-negative tests fail.
