## Phase Goal

**As a** Fava application developer, **I want to** use the README-shaped `fava-simple-groups` capability across one or several host relays, **so that** I retain relay-local authority while reusing ordinary query and publication lifecycles.

<objective>
Project one multi-host QuerySnapshot into exact host-local typed truth, visible disagreement, and event-id-deduplicated content.

Purpose: The app may aggregate forks, but only relay-local records are authoritative; GroupSnapshot must surface, never resolve, conflict.
Output: Approved GroupSnapshot API, same-signer host-conflict corpus, single-host fork choice, and public facade projection evidence.
</objective>
