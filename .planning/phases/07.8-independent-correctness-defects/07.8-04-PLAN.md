<objective>
Make the outbox router read what the engine already knows before it asks a relay, and make its
settled absence come from relay evidence instead of from an object's lifecycle.

Purpose: `RESEARCH.md` Q5 establishes that `router-source-fabricates-empty-initial` has two
independent causes and the engine change fixes only one. The outbox never reads the cache — its
relay-list knowledge comes exclusively from `remember` and from the discovery query's own
results, so route revision 1 is empty even against a fully warm cache. Both causes must land in
the same slice or the finding is closed on a false premise.

`RESEARCH.md` Q6 establishes that coalescing already exists in the engine — registry, admission
window, grouping, and `serves` refcounting — and that the audit's `open_count() == 1` oracle for
`outbox-does-not-coalesce-discovery` becomes the wrong instrument under full engine access. Under
this model there legitimately *are* two observations and one REQ. The finding is re-specified
here against indexer wire frames, not closed silently.

Output: the outbox acquires through the handle, its settled absence is evidence-derived, its
constructor loses the query source, `remember` is gone, and ROUTER-001 has an acceptance that
measures the thing the requirement actually says.

## THE BINDING CONSTRAINT

Unchanged. Everything the outbox does here goes through `RouterQueries::open` and therefore
through `Observer::open`. **If the warm read or the discovery query needs anything the ordinary
query path does not already provide, stop and report.** In particular: do not add a cache-read
shortcut, do not reach for `EventCache` or `QuerySource` directly, and do not give the router a
second way in.
</objective>
