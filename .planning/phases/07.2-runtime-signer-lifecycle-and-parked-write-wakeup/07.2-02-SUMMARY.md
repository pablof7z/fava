## Decisions Made

- Replacement at capacity succeeds because it does not grow the bounded attachment set.
- A Session notification authorizes only an exact-key reload; its global revision never selects or retries another key.
- Provider cancellation cannot establish safety because providers may ignore it; attachment-generation admission does.
