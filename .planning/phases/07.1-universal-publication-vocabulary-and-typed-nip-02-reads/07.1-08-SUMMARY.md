## Decisions Made

- Application paths never construct or publish `WriteIntent`; direct store and provider implementations import it only from `fava-write`.
- Failure preservation compares against a receipt snapshot captured before fault injection rather than re-reading `Write` after the failure and accidentally comparing current state to itself.
- The full semantic failure target is an aggregate Wave 7 gate. Its remaining compile debt stays visible for Plan 09 rather than being hidden by public fields, aliases, or an old publication overload.
