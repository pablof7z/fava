## Decisions Made

- Row classification validates target, non-empty relay, optional petname shape, and extra columns before inserting the key into `seen`; only a complete valid row can make a later complete row duplicate evidence.
- Empty relay text is no hint, missing petname is `None`, present-empty petname is `Some("")`, and petname strings are copied without normalization.
- `ContactList::supersedes` applies same-author Nostr replacement ordering by timestamp and then lower event id.
