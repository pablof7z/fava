## Decisions Made

- A live returned `Write` is the settlement handle for ordinary application publication; a restarted process reattaches using exact durable receipt identity because the original handle no longer exists.
- Subscribe-before-read prevents a receipt transition between initial inspection and observation registration from being missed.
- Neutral `WriteIntent` remains valid only at direct preview, store, and provider boundaries and is imported from `fava-write`, never recovered through the facade.
