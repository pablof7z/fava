## Decisions Made

- Application publication has one vocabulary: payload/edit/event plus `by`, `to`, `publish`, and returned `Write`. Provider implementations continue to use neutral intents directly from their owning crates.
- `WriteRouting` remains public at the facade because removing it would make `Receipt::routing` name a non-importable application result type.
- The external proof no longer previews a constructed intent. It proves zero effects before publication and exact author, destination, event bytes, receipt route, successor, and stale-completion behavior through the actual facade result.
