## Decisions Made

- `all()` accepts mixed terminal destination facts once the route is settled; it does not redefine “all” as “all acknowledgements.”
- `at_least(n)` counts exact retained acknowledgements and rejects zero at predicate construction, before any receipt subscription or wait.
- Unrelated receipt changes are ignored; relevant and lagged notifications cause a complete durable reread before predicate evaluation.
